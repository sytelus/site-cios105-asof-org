Module 6: Digital Media


  Watch this video (2.30 min) and see if you can list all the messages.


This history assignment posted by students at Highland Tech in Anchorage, Alaska, attempts to show current events for the past ten years.  How are they getting their message across?  Who do you think their audience is?  Why?

In this unit, we will explore the ways media is bombarding us with messages; some welcome; some not.

Tour around your village/ community for 1 day and make a list of all the forms of digital media that you see or use to learn.
