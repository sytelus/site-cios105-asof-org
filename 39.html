Module 6: Activity


Participate In collaborative media project : Rethink trash project

http://rethinktrash.wikispaces.com/

Go to “Tutorials and Resources”

Create a public service announcement.  Think about your audience. What message will get through and why.  Use the tools online to create it, and then load it up onto the course site.

How to Create a Public Service Announcement

  1. Using Movie Maker to Create a Public Service Announcement
  2. Wiki How: How to Create a Public Service Announcement
  3. eHow:How to create a Public Service Announcement Film
  4. ReadWriteThink Lesson Plan for Creating a Public Service Announcement
  5. KidVid site: How to create effective videos
  6. Recycle Public Service Announcement

Your public service announcement can be a poster, words (no more than 1 paragraph), powerpoint, video, podcast, etc.

Do: Go to course site and post your work.

www.myvrspot.com/alaska

Reminder:

All posts should be appropriate for public viewing.  Be sure and provide feedback to others who post.  Find 1 thing you liked and 1 thing you feel they could improve on.  Be professional and remember that words may be taken wrong, if you’re not careful on how you say things
