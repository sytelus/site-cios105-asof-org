1.  Creativity and Innovation

Students demonstrate creative thinking, construct knowledge, and develop innovative products and

processes using technology.

 

Students:

a. apply existing knowledge to generate new ideas, products, or processes.

b. create original works as a means of personal or group expression.

c. use models and simulations to explore complex systems and issues.

d. identify trends and forecast possibilities.

NETS*S  1a

Students apply existing knowledge to generate new ideas, products, or processes.

 

• Idea, product, or process used is a result of original thoughts reflecting on something already known, and taking it further.

• Idea, product, or process is used as a solution for a real problem.

 

• Idea, product, or process used is a result of original thoughts reflecting on something already known, and taking it further.

NETS*S  1b

Students create original works as a means of personal or group expression.

 

• Generates a product that expresses an individual or group’s meaning.

• Product inspires others.

 

• Generates a product that expresses an individual or group’s meaning.

 

 

• Describes a product that reflects an original idea.

 

• Recognizes an original idea.

 

 

NETS*S  1c

Students use models and simulations to explore complex systems and issues.

 

• Deconstructs models and simulations to identify parts of complex systems and issues.

2. Communication and Collaboration

Students use digital media and environments to communicate and work collaboratively, including at a distance, to support individual learning and contribute to the learning of others.

 

Students:

a. interact, collaborate, and publish with peers, experts, or others employing a variety of digital environments and media.

b. communicate information and ideas effectively to multiple audiences using a variety of media and formats.

c. develop cultural understanding and global awareness by engaging with learners of other cultures.

d. contribute to project teams to produce original works or solve problems.

 

STANDARD

ADVANCED

PROFICIENT

DEVELOPING

EMERGING

 

NETS*S  2a

Students interact, collaborate, and publish with peers, experts, or others employing a variety of digital environments and media.

 

• Teachers others how to successfully publish digital multimedia in a variety of environments.

 

• Collaborates with peers on current technology tool (i.e., IM, texting, Google docs, Facebook, Open pad, etc.) ideas to produce digital publication.

• Creates digital publications to communicate ideas to an audience and posts for feedback.

• Uses two or more various types of media to communicate message (i.e., podcast, pdf, video, poster, model).

 

• Works together with others using current technology tools to develop a product.

• Creates digital publication.

• Posts digital publication.

• Talks with peers through current technology to discuss a project together.

 

NETS*S  2b

Students communicate information and ideas effectively to multiple audiences using a variety of media and formats.

 

• Speaks on behalf of a formal group (i.e., student representative for school, class, advisory, formal organization) to express ideas and messages to audience.

• Uses technology tools to speak to others (i.e., texting, instant messaging, online forums, Facebook, cell phone, etc.).

• Message changes behavior.

 

 

• Summarizes ideas and information to check for understanding.

• Uses technology tools to speak to others (i.e., texting, instant messaging, online forums, Facebook, cell phone, etc.).

• Uses feedback from audience to improve message.

 

• Uses three or more technology tools to speak to others (i.e., texting, instant messaging, online forums, Facebook, cell phone, etc.).

 

• Uses at least one technology tool to speak to others (i.e., texting, instant messaging, online forums, Facebook, cell phone, etc.).

 

NETS*S  2c

Students develop cultural understanding and global awareness by engaging with learners of other cultures.

 

 

• Using technology tools to talk with others from different cultures around the world.

• Leads a global group, through technology tools, to learn something together about each others’ culture.

 

• Uses technology tools (i.e., email, texting, Facebook, video conferencing, cell phones, online forums, etc.) to interact with others from different cultures.

• Appraises situations to find commonalities between social behavior.

•  Relates ideas of own culture to others.

 

• Begins to see common activities with others from around the world.

• Talks with others from different cultures around the world through technology tools.

• Talks with others from different cultures around the world through technology tools.

 

NETS*S  2d

Students contribute to project teams to produce original works or solve problems.

 

• Presents final project or solution to a problem to a real audience for a real problem.

• Group consists of members who span at least three different geographic locations.

• Group communicates through technology tools.

 

• Creates a product or solves a problem, as a team with a group that communicates through technology tools and spans at least three different geographic locations.

• Collaborates with a group through technology that spans at least three different geographic locations.

• Talks with others from different cultures around the world through technology tools.

Students understand human, cultural, and societal issues related to technology and practice legal and ethical behavior.

 

Students:

a. advocate and practice safe, legal, and responsible use of information and technology.

b. exhibit a positive attitude toward using technology that supports collaboration, learning, and productivity.

c. demonstrate personal responsibility for lifelong learning.

d. exhibit leadership for digital citizenship.

STANDARD

ADVANCED

PROFICIENT

DEVELOPING

EMERGING

Advocate and practice safe, legal, and responsible use of information and technology.

·  Advocates the practice of safe, legal, and responsible use to others and provides examples and mentoring related to same

6. Technology Operations and Concepts

Students demonstrate a sound understanding of technology concepts, systems, and operations.

 

Students:

a. understand and use technology systems.

b. select and use applications effectively and productively.

c. troubleshoot systems and applications.

d. transfer current knowledge to learning of new technologies.

STANDARD

ADVANCED

PROFICIENT

DEVELOPING

EMERGING

 

NETS*S  6a

Students understand and use technology systems.

 

• Teaches others how to use technology systems and why they are relevant in different situations.

• Relates technology concepts to how they are used and why they are used.

• Summarizes concepts into terms that others easily understand who are not technologically literate.

 

• Paraphrases how technology systems work.

• Uses various technology systems and tools.

 

NETS*S  6b

Students select and use applications effectively and productively.

 

• Assists others in selecting and using digital applications.

• Discriminates between applications for most effective uses for specific tasks in three or more situations.

• Creates quality products that are submitted digitally.

• Analyzes applications that allow creation of digital products.

• Uses applications for digital products.

 

NETS*S  6c

Students troubleshoot systems and applications.

 

• Teaches others how trouble shoot and solve problems with system and applications.

• Uses a process to solve problems.

• Evaluates problems with system and application to determine cause.

• Successfully solves the problem in three or more situations.

 

• Determines why software and system doesn’t work properly but fails to solve the problem.

• Tries to help with problems for systems and software.

 

NETS*S  6d

Students transfer current knowledge to learning of new technologies.

 

• Publishes ‘how to’ use new technologies for others to learn from.

• Compares and contrasts old and new technology.

• Explains how to use concepts already known to apply with new technology for success.

 

• Uses new technologies and can explain the changes.

• Explores new technologies.
