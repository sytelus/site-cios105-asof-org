Module 1: Introduction



Generally, ‘literacy’ is associated with reading.  To be literate means that you can read.  The title of this course though is "Computer Literacy."  What do you think that means?


How will you manage your time?  Have you thought about ways you will modify your daily schedule to allow for one hour a day to work on this class?  Do you have a spot to work that is quite and allows you to get things done?




Assignment #1: Introduce Yourself


Go to the online forum and post 3 things about yourself:

1) First name only and town you live in.

2) Why you think this class will help you (minimum of 4 sentences).

3) Respond to at least 2 other people posted. 



Reminder:

All posts should be appropriate for public viewing.  Be sure and provide feedback to others who post.  Find 1 thing you liked and 1 thing you feel they could improve on.  Be professional and remember that words may be taken wrong, if you’re not careful on how you say things.
